My WhatsApp extension privacy

Updated 9 October 2026. This extension lets CRM users share their selected property files through their own linked WhatsApp Web account after reviewing and confirming the sender, recipient, and files.

Data used

The extension receives the selected property photos, videos, optional brochure, property identifiers, captions, recipient number, and requesting CRM origin. It reads the currently linked WhatsApp account number to display and verify the sender. It does not read your personal address book or collect your chat history.

Storage and sharing

Prepared attachments are stored temporarily in this browser profile’s extension-local IndexedDB. They are removed on completion or cancellation, and expire after ten minutes. Expiry cleanup runs while the browser is running or when the extension next checks pending requests; closing the browser does not instantly erase pending data.

Confirmed files and messages are sent to WhatsApp for the recipient you selected. Personal WhatsApp session credentials are not uploaded to the Realty Pandit server. The extension has no analytics, advertising, or separate developer collection endpoint. Normal CRM authentication, media access, and brochure generation still use Realty Pandit’s servers.

Permissions

Access to approved CRM origins carries the selected share request. WhatsApp Web access and scripting enable account verification and sending with the packaged WA-JS library. Alarms clean up expired requests. Sending requires confirmation on the extension-owned review page.

Your controls

Cancel the review to discard pending files. You can disable or uninstall the extension through Chrome’s extensions settings. Removing the extension does not delete messages already sent to WhatsApp. The integration is unofficial and can stop working or risk WhatsApp account restrictions.

For privacy questions, contact your Realty Pandit CRM administrator through the company’s normal support channel.
